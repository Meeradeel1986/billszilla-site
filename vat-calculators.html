VAT & GST Calculators by Country

One free calculator per country, with that country's standard VAT or GST rate pre-filled. Add tax to a price or strip it out of a total — for 61 top countries, in local currency.

There is no single world VAT rate: each country sets its own, from 5% to 27%, with its own reduced rates and exemptions. Pick your country below — the calculator opens with that country's standard rate already entered, and you can type in a different rate if your item qualifies for one.

Two systems to know: most countries call it VAT; India, Australia, Canada, Singapore, New Zealand and Pakistan call the same idea GST. Japan calls it consumption tax, and Malaysia uses SST instead of VAT. The calculators use each country's own term.

VAT & GST calculators FAQ

Value Added Tax (VAT) and Goods and Services Tax (GST) are consumption taxes charged on most goods and services. GST is the same idea under a different name, used in countries such as India, Australia, Canada and Singapore.
Each country sets its own standard rate — from 5% in the UAE and Oman to 27% in Hungary — and its own reduced rates, exemptions and registration rules. This hub lists the standard rate for each of the 61 countries, verified in October 2026.
