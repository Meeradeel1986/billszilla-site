## Hero-photo rule (from 2026-10-25 — mirrors PDFZippy's blog setup)
Every article MUST have a photographic hero image: generate one on the article's topic
(invoice/freelance/small-business/office theme, no readable text in the image), save as
assets/blog/<slug>.jpg (~1200px wide, under ~150KB), and use it in THREE places: the
article's <figure class="hero">, its og:image meta + JSON-LD image, and on /blog/ index
(featured slot if newest, else a grid card). Template: blog/article-template.html.
Never publish an article without its hero photo.
