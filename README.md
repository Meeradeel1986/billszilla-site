# BillsZilla — Free Invoice Generator

**▶ Live site: [https://billszilla.com](https://billszilla.com)**

BillsZilla is a free invoice generator for freelancers and small businesses worldwide. Create a professional invoice, quote, receipt, or proforma in your browser and download or print it as a PDF — **no sign-up, no watermark, nothing uploaded**. Your data stays in your browser.

## Features

- **Four documents** — Invoice, Quote, Receipt, and Proforma invoice makers
- **6 templates**, including a classic Zoho-style layout, with your business logo on the PDF
- **50+ currencies** and 8 languages, including Urdu and Arabic (RTL)
- Extras: payment QR code ("Scan to pay"), "Paid" stamp, amount in words, optional due date
- **Free tools** — Payment Reminder Generator, Hourly Rate Calculator, Time Tracker, Tax Calculator (VAT/GST/sales tax, add or remove)
- **Invoice templates** — 6 designs × Word / Excel / PDF downloads (Excel files carry live formulas)
- Guides for freelancers, contractors, photographers, consultants, designers, and cleaning services

## How it works

- Static site (HTML/CSS/JS) hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**, auto-deployed from the `main` branch of this repo — no build step, no backend.
- Invoices are generated entirely client-side; nothing leaves the visitor's device.
- Anonymous, aggregate usage counts only — see the site's [Privacy Policy](https://billszilla.com/privacy/).
