Cleaning Service Invoice Template

Per-visit, recurring and deep-clean billing for homes and offices. A free cleaning service invoice template you can send in seconds.

Cleaning businesses run on repeat work — weekly homes, monthly offices, one-off deep cleans — and the invoicing has to be just as routine. Clients (and property managers especially) want to see the visit dates, whether each clean was charged per visit or per hour, and whether supplies were included, all without having to ask. What to include on a cleaning service invoice

  • Your business name, contact details and tax ID if registered
  • Client name and the service address being cleaned
  • The billing period and the actual visit dates
  • Per-visit charges and hourly charges on separate lines
  • Deep cleans, oven/fridge cleans and extras itemised apart from regular visits
  • Whether supplies and equipment are included or charged
  • Recurring schedule (weekly / fortnightly / monthly) the invoice covers
  • Payment terms — weekly clients often pay per visit, monthly accounts Net 7
  • Your payment details — bank transfer or payment link
  • Cancellation or reschedule policy summary

Why monthly invoicing wins for regular cleaning clients

Chasing $85 after every visit wastes more time than the cleaning some weeks. Moving regular clients to one monthly invoice — like the sample — cuts admin to minutes, gives clients a single predictable bill, and makes you look like the established business you are. Itemise the visit dates on it and there is nothing to query. For one-off work (deep cleans, end-of-tenancy), invoice on completion with payment due on receipt. New to setting rates?
