Provinces and territories

Canada's 5% GST is only half the story in most provinces: five provinces blend it into a single HST, four add a separate provincial tax, and Alberta and the three territories charge GST only. How to calculate Canada GST in 3 steps

  1. Enter your amount. Type the price you are working with — either before tax or tax-inclusive, depending on what you have.
  2. Pick the rate and direction. The 5% standard rate is pre-filled and editable; choose “Add tax” or “Remove tax”.
  3. Read the split instantly. You get the net amount, the exact tax, and the gross total — ready to quote, invoice, or check a receipt against.

Why getting the Canada split right matters

Tax is the part of the price most people get wrong, because the headline rate is rarely the whole story: reduced rates, zero-rating and exemptions all change what applies to a given item. This calculator starts from the standard rate — and if your item qualifies for a different rate, type it in and it calculates that instead.
