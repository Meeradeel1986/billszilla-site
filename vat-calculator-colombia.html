How to calculate Colombia VAT in 3 steps

  1. Enter your amount. Type the price you are working with — either before tax or tax-inclusive, depending on what you have.
  2. Pick the rate and direction. The 19% standard rate is pre-filled and editable; choose “Add tax” or “Remove tax”.
  3. Read the split instantly. You get the net amount, the exact tax, and the gross total — ready to quote, invoice, or check a receipt against.

Why getting the Colombia split right matters

Whether you are a seller working out what to charge or a buyer checking a receipt, the split matters: net, tax, total. Get any one of them wrong and either you absorb the tax or your customer disputes the invoice. This calculator shows all three instantly, in your country's currency.
