How to use a template in 3 steps

  1. Pick a design and format. Choose the template that fits your brand, then download Word for editing, Excel for automatic totals, or PDF for print.
  2. Make it yours. Replace the sample business and client details, list your work with quantities and rates, and set your tax rate.
  3. Send it and get paid. Email the file or attach it to a message. Next time, save the typing — the online generator remembers your business details.
