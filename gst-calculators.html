GST Calculators by Country (2026)

GST is the same idea as VAT under a different name — Australia, New Zealand, India, Pakistan, Singapore and Canada all call it GST. Pick your country below; its rate is pre-filled.

Most countries call it VAT; a handful call the same tax GST. The calculators below cover every country that calls it GST, with the standard rate already entered — type in a different rate if your item qualifies for one. Countries that call it VAT are on the VAT calculators page instead.

Two systems to know: most GST countries charge one flat national rate. Canada is the exception: the federal rate is 5% GST, and each province either adds its own tax or folds it into a single HST — so the combined rate runs from 5% to 15% depending on the province.
