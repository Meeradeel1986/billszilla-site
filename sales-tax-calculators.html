US Sales Tax Calculators

One free calculator per state, with the state's base rate pre-filled. Add tax to a price or strip it out of a total — for all 50 states plus Washington, DC.

There is no federal sales tax in the US: each state sets its own rate, and counties and cities often add more on top. Pick your state below — the calculator opens with that state's base rate already entered, and you can type in a combined local rate if you know it.

Five states charge no statewide sales tax: Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon. Only Alaska lets local governments add their own general sales tax.
