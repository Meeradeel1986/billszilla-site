How to calculate your rate in 3 steps

  1. Set your income goal. Enter what you want to earn in a year — be honest about the lifestyle you're working toward.
  2. Add expenses and time off. Include software, hardware, taxes, and the weeks you won't bill. The calculator subtracts non-billable reality.
  3. Get your rate instantly. Your income goal plus expenses, divided by real billable hours — that's the rate that actually sustains you.

Why most freelancers undercharge

The classic mistake is dividing a salary by 2,080 hours. You won't bill 40 hours a week — meetings, admin, marketing and downtime eat 30–50%.
